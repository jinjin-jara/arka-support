# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

arka does not collect personal information.

## What is stored

The links, notes, collections, and cover photos you save are stored **only on your device**.
arka has no server, and the developer cannot access any of it.

When you move to another device, you export and import a backup file yourself. That file is saved
only where you choose to put it.

## What is sent

To fetch the title and preview image of a link you saved, arka makes a request to **that link's own
server** (for example, saving an Instagram post means a request to Instagram). Only the link address
is sent. Your notes and anything else you saved are not sent.

arka has no server of its own. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

## What is not collected

- Accounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers
- Location
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA), usage history, analytics
- Crash reports

arka uses no third-party SDKs and no analytics tools.

## Permissions

arka does not ask for system permissions such as photo library or location access.

When you choose a cover photo, arka uses the system photo picker. That does not reach your whole
library — only the one photo you pick is handed to the app.

When you tap the paste button, the operating system tells you about, or asks about, clipboard access.
The clipboard is read only at that moment.

On Android, internet access is used to fetch link titles and previews.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
A backup file you exported stays where you put it, so delete it yourself if you want it gone.
