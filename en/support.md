# arka Support

A place to keep the links you liked. How to use it is written up in the [guide](/arka-support/en/guide).

## Common questions

**Where are my saved links kept?**
On your device. arka has no server, and nothing you save leaves the device.
To move to another device, export a backup from Settings and take that file with you — the same file
works between iPhone and Android.

**arka isn't in my share sheet.**
Tap `···` (More) at the bottom of the share sheet and turn arka on in the list.

**Thumbnails for Instagram or Threads links don't show up.**
Those pages need a login, so a preview can't be fetched. Long-press the card and use
`Choose a cover photo` to put in a photo yourself.

**A thumbnail that used to show is gone.**
Some sites put an expiry time in the image address. Settings → `Fetch thumbnails again` brings them back,
and once fetched the image is stored on your device.

**I want to send a collection to someone.**
In the collections view, long-press a folder and choose `Share`. The links become plain text.
The person receiving it can load it with `Import from notes` (`Import from text` on iPhone).

**I want to back up what I saved.**
Settings → `Export backup`. One file holds your links, notes, collections, and cover photos.
